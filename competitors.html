You are already winning this market. The relevant question is not who is beating you — it is how long the door stays open.

We checked every guttering and roofline firm we could reach around Taunton and Bridgwater. None has a Meta pixel. None is advertising. Only one has a tag manager. The Meta ads trading under "Gutter Doctor" belong to unrelated firms in Falkirk and Sudbury, not to you.

The One Worth Watching
Pepper Roofing, Bridgwater — smaller than you, and doing three things you are not
  • Seventeen pages to your nine, and they name Taunton on them. They are advertising into your town.
  • AggregateRating markup with seven reviews on the page, so their stars are eligible to appear in search results. Yours are not, on a business many times their size.
  • Google Tag Manager, which means somebody is at least able to add and manage tracking properly.

They are not beating you and they are not close. But they are the only firm here behaving like they intend to grow, and everything they have done is cheap and copyable — which cuts both ways.

Where You Are Already Ahead
Four things that are true of you and of almost nobody else here
  • You publish a landline. Two of the three rivals give a mobile only. For commercial and letting-agent work, a 01823 number reads as a business rather than a man with a van — and that is where the larger jobs live.
  • You have analytics and they mostly do not. Only Pepper has anything comparable.
  • Your titles and schema are properly done. Two competitors publish nothing but a WebSite tag.
  • Nine service pages, each with real copy — bargeboards, cladding, painting, fascias and soffits. Byron's has three pages in total.
And the honest read on what that means

You are booked nine weeks out at 50% margins in a market where nobody is advertising, nobody is retargeting, and one competitor has review markup. That is not a coincidence — you are winning a race almost nobody has entered.

The risk is not Pepper Roofing. It is a national roofline firm or a franchise deciding Somerset is worth buying, walking into an auction with no incumbent bidder and taking the top of the market in a season. Right now you would have no pixel, no ad account history and no attribution to defend with.

What We Could Not Check
Marked honestly rather than guessed
  • Two local firms were unreachable — PW Maintenance & Roofing did not resolve, GBC Maintenance returned an access block. Neither appears in the table.
  • Everyone's Google Business Profile, review counts and map pack position, including yours. That is where a lot of local work is actually won and we have no reliable way to measure it.
  • Anyone's revenue, job values, pricing, margins or lead volume, including yours — your figures are as you reported them.
  • Whether any of these firms advertise on Google, which is a separate auction we have not assessed.
  • Checkatrade, Which? Trusted Trader and similar directories, which may be where your rivals are actually spending.

An empty auction is an opportunity right up until somebody else notices it.

You have the margin and the reputation to take this market properly. Nobody local is in a position to stop you.
